From the given equation \(f + 3z = 11\), solve for \(f\) to obtain \(f = 11 - 3z\). Multiplying the same equation through by \(3\) yields \(3f + 9z = 33\).

From the second given equation \(3(f - 1) - 5z = -68\), expand and rearrange to obtain \(3f - 5z = -65\).

Subtract this last equation from \(3f + 9z = 33\):
\[
(3f + 9z) - (3f - 5z) = 33 - (-65) \implies 14z = 98.
\]
Hence \(z = 7\). Substitute into \(f = 11 - 3z\) to conclude \(f = -10\).

Thus \(f = -10 \land z = 7\).
