The function under consideration is \(f(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{119}|kx-1|\). Equivalently, \(f(x)=\sum_{k=1}^{119}k|x-1/k|\). Thus a minimizer of \(f\) is a weighted median of the points \(\{1/k\}_{k=1}^{119}\) with respective weights \(k\).

The total weight is \(7140\), so any weighted median must have cumulative weight at least \(3570\) on each side. The right-hand partial sum \(\sum_{k=85}^{119}k=3570\) therefore forces every minimizer to lie in the interval \([1/85,1/84]\).

The slope of \(f\) increases by \(2k\) at each kink \(1/k\). In particular the slope on \((-\infty,1/119)\) equals \(-7140\), and the same increment rule shows that the slope vanishes throughout the closed interval \([1/85,1/84]\). Convexity of \(f\) then implies that the constant value of \(f\) on this interval is the global minimum.

It remains only to evaluate the constant. At the left endpoint one has
\[
|kx-1|=\begin{cases}
1-k/85 & (k\le84),\\
0 & (k=85),\\
k/85-1 & (k\ge86).
\end{cases}
\]
Hence
\[
f(1/85)=\sum_{k=1}^{84}(1-k/85)+\sum_{k=86}^{119}(k/85-1).
\]
The coefficients of \(1/85\) cancel, leaving the constant \(84-35=49\). The same arithmetic may be rewritten
\[
f(1/85)=\sum_{k=1}^{84}|k/85-1|+|1-1|+\sum_{k=86}^{119}|k/85-1|=84-3570/85+3485/85-34=49.
\]
Thus the minimum value of \(f\) is \(49\).
