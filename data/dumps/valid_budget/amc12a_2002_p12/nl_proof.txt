The equation \(x^2-63x+k=0\) has prime roots \(p\) and \(q\), so \(p+q=63\) and \(k=pq\). One of these roots must be even, hence equal to \(2\). Since \(61\) is prime, the roots are exactly \(2\) and \(61\). Thus the only possible value of \(k\) is \(122\), and the equation \(x^2-63x+122=0\) indeed has both roots prime. Therefore there is exactly one possible value of \(k\).
