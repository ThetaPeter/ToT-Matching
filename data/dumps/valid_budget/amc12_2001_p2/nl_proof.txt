Let \(N\) be a two-digit number, written \(N=10a+b\) with \(a\in\{1,\dots,9\}\) and \(b\in\{0,\dots,9\}\), satisfying the given equation \(10a+b=a\cdot b+a+b\).

Rearranging the equation immediately yields the equivalent relation \(9a=a\cdot b\).

Since \(a\neq 0\), it follows that \(b=9\).

Hence the units digit of \(N\) is \(9\).
