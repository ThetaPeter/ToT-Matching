By the given identity, for all real \(x,y\) we have
\[
(xy-1)^2+(x+y)^2=(x^2+1)(y^2+1).
\]
The right-hand side expands as \(x^2y^2+x^2+y^2+1\), which is identical to \((1+x^2)(1+y^2)\).  
For every real \(z\) one has \(z^2+1\ge1\), so both \(1+x^2\ge1\) and \(1+y^2\ge1\). Their product is therefore at least \(1\), which yields
\[
(xy-1)^2+(x+y)^2\ge1
\]
for all real \(x,y\).  
Direct substitution shows that the value \(1\) is attained at \(x=y=0\). Consequently the minimum of the expression is exactly \(1\).
