By \(\ell_3\), the first \(2003\) even positive integers are precisely the terms \(2k\) for \(k=1,\dots,2003\) and the first \(2003\) odd positive integers are precisely the terms \(2k-1\) for the same range of \(k\).

Apply \(\ell_1\) with \(n=2003\): the sum of those even integers equals \(2003\cdot 2004\).

Apply \(\ell_2\) with \(n=2003\): the sum of those odd integers equals \(2003^2\).

Their difference is therefore \(2003\cdot 2004-2003^2\). By \(\mathrm{tc}_1\) this difference equals \(2003\), which is exactly the identity \(\mathrm{ts}_1\).
