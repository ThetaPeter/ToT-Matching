### Setup and elementary inequality.
Let \(a,b\) be arbitrary real numbers satisfying \(a^2+b^2=2\).  For any such reals we have the elementary nonnegativity \((a-b)^2\ge0\), which rearranges immediately to \(a^2+b^2\ge2ab\).

### Combining with the given constraint.
Substituting the given equality \(a^2+b^2=2\) into the preceding inequality yields \(2\ge2ab\).

### Scaling.
Dividing the last relation by the positive constant \(2\) produces the equivalent inequality \(1\ge ab\).

### Conclusion.
The relation \(1\ge ab\) is identical to \(ab\le1\), as required.
