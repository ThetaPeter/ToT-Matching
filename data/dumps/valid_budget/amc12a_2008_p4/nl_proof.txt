Let \(P=\prod_{k=2}^{502}\frac{4k}{4(k-1)}\). Canceling the constant factor of \(4\) in each term of the product immediately yields
\[
P=\prod_{k=2}^{502}\frac{k}{k-1}.
\]
The resulting product is telescoping:
\[
P=\frac{2}{1}\cdot\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{4}{3}\cdots\frac{502}{501}=502.
\]
Hence \(P=502\).
