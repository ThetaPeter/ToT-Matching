Given integers \(x>y>0\) satisfying \(x+y+xy=80\), the identity \(x+y+xy=80\) rearranges at once to \((x+1)(y+1)=81\). The positive divisors of \(81\) are precisely \(\{1,3,9,27,81\}\). Among these the unique ordered pair of integers \(a>b>1\) with product \(81\) is \((27,3)\). Setting \(x+1=27\) and \(y+1=3\) therefore yields \(x=26\) and \(y=2\), so in particular \(x=26\).
