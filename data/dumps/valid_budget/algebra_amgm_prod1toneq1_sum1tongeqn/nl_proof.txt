The given support core consists solely of the claim \(\mathrm{ts}_1\), which has empty dependencies. Consequently the argument is simply the assertion of that claim:

Let \(a\) be any real-valued function on the natural numbers satisfying \(a_i\ge 0\) for every \(i\in\mathbb{N}\). If
\[
\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}a_i=1,
\]
then
\[
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_i\ge n.
\]
