Assume \(|x-2|=p\) and \(x<2\). Then \(|x-2|=2-x\), so \(x=2-p\). Substituting yields \(x-p=(2-p)-p\), hence \(x-p=2-2p\). This identity together with the given multiple-choice options shows that the correct selection is (C). Moreover, whenever \(p\geq 0\) the same identity \(x-p=2-2p\) holds for every \(x<2\). Combining these facts shows that \(x-p=2-2p\) with choice (C), and that the identity does not hold in every case (so (E) is not always true).
