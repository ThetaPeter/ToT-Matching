### Setup.
Let \(a\) and \(b\) be arbitrary complex numbers. We establish the identity \((a+a)(a+b)=2a^{2}+2ab\).

### Proof.
We have the elementary identity \(a+a=2a\). Substituting this factor on the left-hand side immediately yields
\[
(a+a)(a+b)=2a(a+b).
\]
Distributing the remaining factor of \(2a\) then gives
\[
2a(a+b)=2a^{2}+2ab.
\]
Combining the two displayed equalities produces
\[
(a+a)(a+b)=2a^{2}+2ab,
\]
as required. (The same chain of equalities holds for any complex \(a,b\).)
