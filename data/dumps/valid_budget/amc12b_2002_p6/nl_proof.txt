Assume \(a,b\in\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}\) are the roots of the quadratic \(x^2+ax+b\). By Vieta’s formulas the product of the roots equals the constant term, so \(ab=b\). Since \(b\neq0\) it follows that \(a=1\). Likewise the sum of the roots equals the negation of the linear coefficient, so \(a+b=-a\). Substituting the already-established value \(a=1\) yields \(1+b=-1\), hence \(b=-2\). Therefore \((a,b)=(1,-2)\).
