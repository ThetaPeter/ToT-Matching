The sequence \(b_n\) satisfies the linear recurrence \(b_n=2b_{n-1}-b_{n-2}\). The characteristic equation of this recurrence has a repeated root of \(1\), so the general term takes the form \(b_n=A+Bn\). Matching initial conditions then yields the unique coefficients \(A=-1/3\) and \(B=4/3\). Substituting these values produces \(b_n=(4n-1)/3\), and therefore \(a_n=3/(4n-1)\).
