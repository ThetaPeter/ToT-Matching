Given that \(x\), \(y\), and \(z\) are positive reals satisfying \(xyz=1\), \(x+1/z=5\), and \(y+1/x=29\).

It follows that \(x(1+y)=5\). It also follows that \(x=1/(29-y)\). Substituting the latter into the former therefore yields
\[
\frac{1+y}{29-y}=5.
\]
Solving this equation gives \(y=24\). Substituting this value of \(y\) then gives \(x=1/5\). Combined with \(xyz=1\), these values of \(x\) and \(y\) yield \(z=5/24\).

Hence
\[
z+\frac{1}{y}=\frac14.
\]
