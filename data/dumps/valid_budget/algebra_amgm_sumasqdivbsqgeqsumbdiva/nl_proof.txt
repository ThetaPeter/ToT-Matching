Assume \(a>0\), \(b>0\) and \(c>0\).

From this it follows that
\[
\frac{a^2}{b^2}+\frac{b^2}{c^2}\ge 2\frac{a}{c}.
\]
It also follows that the substitutions \(x=\frac{a}{b}\), \(y=\frac{b}{c}\) and \(z=\frac{c}{a}\) satisfy \(xyz=1\).

Combining the preceding facts yields
\[
\frac{a^2}{b^2}+\frac{b^2}{c^2}+\frac{c^2}{a^2}\ge\frac{b}{a}+\frac{c}{b}+\frac{a}{c}.
\]
