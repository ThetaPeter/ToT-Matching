By the finite arithmetico-geometric summation formula,
\[
\sum_{k=1}^n k r^k=\frac{r\bigl(1-(n+1)r^n+n r^{n+1}\bigr)}{(1-r)^2}.
\]
Substituting the purely imaginary value \(r=i\) therefore yields the explicit expression
\[
S(n,i)=\frac{i\bigl(1-(n+1)i^n+n i^{n+1}\bigr)}{(1-i)^2}.
\]
Since \(i^4=1\), the successive powers of \(i\) (and hence the summands \(k i^k\)) are periodic of period \(4\). Consequently \(S\) is completely determined by the residue class of \(n\) modulo \(4\).

In the particular arithmetic progression \(n=4m+1\) the closed form simplifies to
\[
\operatorname{Re}(S)=\frac{n+1}{2},\qquad\operatorname{Im}(S)=-\frac{n-1}{2}.
\]
The integer \(n=97\) belongs to this progression, and the preceding identities therefore give \(S=48+49i\). Combined with the given biconditional
\[
\sum_{k=1}^n k i^k=48+49i\iff n=97,
\]
the required evaluation follows.
