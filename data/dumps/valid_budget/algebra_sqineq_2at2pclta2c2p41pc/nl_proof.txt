Let \(a,c\in\mathbb{R}\). The difference between the two sides of the desired inequality is
\[
\bigl(a^2+c^2+4(1+c)\bigr)-2a(2+c)=(a-2-c)^2.
\]
Consequently the inequality holds if and only if \((a-c-2)^2\ge0\). The latter identity is true for every pair of real numbers, and therefore
\[
\forall a,c\in\mathbb{R},\qquad 2a(2+c)\le a^2+c^2+4(1+c).
\]
