Given \(a\geq b>1\). Set \(t:=\log_a b\). Then \(0<t\leq 1\). The expression under consideration equals \(2-(t+1/t)\). For every \(t>0\) one has \(t+1/t\geq 2\), with equality if and only if \(t=1\). Consequently the expression is at most \(0\), and this value \(0\) is attained (hence is the maximum).
