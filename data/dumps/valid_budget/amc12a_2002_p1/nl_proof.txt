By factoring out the common term \(2x+3\), we have the identity
\[
(2x+3)(x-4)+(2x+3)(x-6)=(2x+3)(2x-10).
\]
Consequently the equation
\[
(2x+3)(x-4)+(2x+3)(x-6)=0
\]
is equivalent to
\[
(2x+3)(2x-10)=0.
\]
The roots of the latter equation are \(x=-3/2\) and \(x=5\), whose sum is \(7/2\). Therefore the sum of the roots of the original equation is likewise \(7/2\).
