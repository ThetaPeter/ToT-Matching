From the given relations \(a_n=\tan(\theta_n)\) and \(\theta_{n+2}=\theta_n+\theta_{n+1}\), the initial values \(\theta_1=\pi/4\) and \(\theta_2=\pi/6\) yield the closed form
\[
\theta_n=F_{n-2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{4}+F_{n-3}\cdot\frac{\pi}{6}.
\]
Specializing at \(n=2009\) therefore produces
\[
a_{2009}=\tan\Bigl(F_{2007}\frac{\pi}{4}+F_{2006}\frac{\pi}{6}\Bigr).
\]
The tangent of that argument vanishes if and only if \(12\) divides \(3F_{2007}+2F_{2006}\). Combined with the congruences \(F_{2007}\equiv0\pmod{12}\) and \(F_{2008}\equiv0\pmod{12}\), the same closed form implies that \(\theta_{2009}\) is an integer multiple of \(\pi\). Consequently \(\lvert a_{2009}\rvert=0\).
