We start from the given equation \(10^x \cdot 100^{2x} = 1000^5\). Since \(100 = 10^2\) and \(1000 = 10^3\), substitution yields \(10^x \cdot (10^2)^{2x} = (10^3)^5\). The right-hand side simplifies as \((10^3)^5 = 10^{15}\), so the equation becomes \(10^x \cdot 10^{4x} = 10^{15}\). Combining the powers of \(10\) on the left-hand side produces \(10^{5x} = 10^{15}\). Equating exponents therefore gives \(5x = 15\), and solving for \(x\) yields \(x = 3\). Hence the value of \(x\) is \(3\).
