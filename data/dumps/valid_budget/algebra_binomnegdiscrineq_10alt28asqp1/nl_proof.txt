### Setup.
Let \(a\) be an arbitrary real number. The desired inequality is equivalent to
\[
28a^2-10a+1\ge0.
\]
(The original claim is precisely this statement.)

### Discriminant.
The quadratic \(28x^2-10x+1\) has discriminant
\[
(-10)^2-4\cdot28\cdot1=100-112=-12<0.
\]
Since the leading coefficient \(28>0\), the quadratic is positive for every real \(x\). In particular it is nonnegative at \(x=a\), which is the claimed inequality.

(The single node of the given support core is exactly the statement just established.)
