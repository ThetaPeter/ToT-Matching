From the given identity
\[
\sqrt[a]{\bigl(N\sqrt[b]{\bigl(N\sqrt[c]{N}\bigr)}\bigr)}=\sqrt[36]{N^{25}},
\]
rewrite the left-hand side by successive extraction of roots to obtain
\[
N^{[1+(1+1/c)/b]/a}.
\]
Equating exponents with the right-hand side therefore yields
\[
\frac{1+(1+1/c)/b}{a}=\frac{25}{36}.
\]
Clearing the remaining denominators produces the Diophantine relation
\[
36(bc+c+1)=25abc,
\]
which may equivalently be written
\[
36\bigl[(b+1)c+1\bigr]=25abc.
\]
The first form immediately implies the congruence
\[
bc+c+1\equiv0\pmod{25}.
\]
Combined with the same relation it also forces
\[
36\mid abc.
\]
The only triple of positive integers \(a,b,c\) satisfying the last three conditions simultaneously is \(a=4\), \(b=3\), \(c=6\). In particular \(b=3\).
