We are given that the volume of the small cube is \(1\). It follows that the side length of the small cube is \(1\). The surface area of the small cube is therefore \(6\). The surface area of the large cube is twice the surface area of the small cube, hence equal to \(12\). Consequently the square of the side length of the large cube equals \(2\), so that side length is \(\sqrt{2}\). The volume of the large cube is therefore \(2\sqrt{2}\).
