We work from the given form \(f(t)=9t^2+3t+1\). Substituting the argument \(3z\) into the equation \(f(3z)=7\) therefore yields
\[
9(3z)^2+3(3z)+1=7,
\]
which simplifies at once to
\[
81z^2+9z+1=7.
\]
Rearrangement produces the quadratic equation
\[
81z^2+9z-6=0.
\]
For a quadratic \(az^2+bz+c=0\) the sum of the roots is \(-b/a\). Applying this identity to the coefficients \(a=81\) and \(b=9\) gives
\[
-\frac{9}{81}=-\frac19.
\]
Hence the sum of all \(z\) satisfying \(f(3z)=7\) equals \(-1/9\).
