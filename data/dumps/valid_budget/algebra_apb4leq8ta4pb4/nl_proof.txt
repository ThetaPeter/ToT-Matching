We work throughout with the given claims only.

By claim \(\mathrm{l4}\), \((a+b)^2\le 2(a^2+b^2)\) holds for all real \(a,b\). By claim \(\mathrm{l2}\), \((a^2+b^2)^2\le 2(a^4+b^4)\) holds for all real \(a,b\). Combining these two inequalities yields claim \(\mathrm{l5}\): \((a+b)^4\le 8(a^4+b^4)\) for all real \(a,b\).

Independently, claim \(\mathrm{l6}\) asserts that \((a+b)^2\le 2(a^2+b^2)\) for all real \(a,b\). Squaring both sides of this inequality (which are nonnegative) immediately produces claim \(\mathrm{l7}\): \((a+b)^4\le 8(a^4+b^4)\) for all real \(a,b\).

Restricting the universal statements \(\mathrm{l5}\) and \(\mathrm{l7}\) to the case of positive real numbers \(a\) and \(b\) yields the desired inequality of claim \(\mathrm{ts_1}\).
